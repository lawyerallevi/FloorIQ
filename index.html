The Problem

Brand training is broken.
Everywhere.

Premium retail brands invest everything in their customer experience. Then they hand new staff a PDF and hope for the best.

01
Brand standards live in documents no one reads. A beautifully crafted 60-page manual is not a training programme. It is a filing cabinet.
02
