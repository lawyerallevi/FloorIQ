The Process

From brand manual
to trained team
in 72 hours.

No implementation project. No consultant. No content team. Just upload, assign, and watch your team grow.

Upload your brand document

Drag and drop any PDF — your brand standards guide, seasonal lookbook, product knowledge manual, or service excellence handbook. FloorIQ accepts documents up to 200 pages. Multiple documents can be combined into a single training programme.

⏱ Takes 2 minutes
2
AI generates your scenario library

FloorIQ's RAG engine reads your document, identifies key product knowledge, service standards, and brand values — then generates 20 to 50 realistic customer interaction scenarios. Questions

Everything you
want to know.

If your question is not here, use the contact form and we will reply within one business day.

Do I need a technical team to set up FloorIQ?
+
No. FloorIQ is designed for retail managers, not IT departments. You log in, upload a PDF, and the AI does the rest. The entire setup from account creation to your first training scenario takes less than 15 minutes. No integrations required, no software to install.
What types of documents can I upload?
+
FloorIQ accepts PDF, Word (.docx), and PowerPoint (.pptx) files up to 200 pages. You can upload brand standards manuals, product knowledge guides, seasonal lookbooks, service excellence frameworks, compliance documents, and visual merchandising guidelines. Multiple documents can be combined into a single training programme.
How does the AI know what good answers look like?
+
The scoring model is trained on your specific document. It understands what your brand considers correct, incomplete, or off-brand. Scoring is not based on generic customer service principles — it is based on your standards. Managers can also review and adjust scoring criteria for any scenario if the AI misses a nuance specific to their business.
Is our brand content secure?
+
Yes. All documents are encrypted at rest and in transit. Your brand content is never used to train shared AI models or shared with other clients. Each organisation's data is completely isolated. FloorIQ is registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller and operates under GDPR-compliant data handling practices.
What languages does FloorIQ support?
+
FloorIQ currently supports English, French, and Arabic — covering the core needs of most international flagship and travel retail operations. Additional languages including Spanish, Mandarin, and Japanese are on the roadmap for 2025. Training scenarios are generated in the language of your choice and staff can switch languages within a single session.
How is FloorIQ different from a generic LMS like Axonify or WorkRamp?
+
Generic LMS platforms require you to create all training content manually — writing questions, building modules, recording videos. That takes months and a dedicated L&D team. FloorIQ generates training from your existing documents automatically. It is also built specifically for luxury and premium retail, meaning the scenarios reflect real floor interactions — not generic customer service situations. At £199/month per store — a flat fee regardless of team size — it is designed for single stores and small teams, not just enterprise rollouts.
Can I try it before committing?
+
We offer a free demonstration using your own brand document — so you can see exactly what FloorIQ generates from your specific content before making any decision. We are currently in our founding cohort phase, working closely with a small number of pilot clients to refine the platform. If you are interested, please use the contact form to request a demo.
What happens when we launch a new collection or update our brand standards?
+
Simply upload the new document. FloorIQ generates a fresh set of scenarios in under 5 minutes. You can then assign the new scenarios to your full team or just to specific people who need refreshing. Previous training history is preserved so you can compare performance before and after the update. Seasonal refreshes take minutes, not weeks.
